The Three Rivers STEM & Beyond Summer Camp has officially come to a successful close!

Our week-long STEM adventure flew by! A huge thank-you to our amazing student volunteers, who made Math, Chess, Science, Coding, and Chemistry both educational and fun. The camp was packed with engaging activities and valuable learning!

Camp Highlights:

  • Hybrid learning: Online instruction combined with in-person activities for a seamless learning experience
  • Learning through fun: Interactive games kept students actively involved and encouraged creative thinking
  • A rewarding experience: Each student received a special reward to celebrate their participation

We sincerely appreciate the support of all the parents and community members!

As the new school year approaches, we wish every student academic success and continued growth. We look forward to seeing everyone again at our next event!
